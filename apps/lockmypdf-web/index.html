lockmypdf brings your PDF work and your privacy together, turning every document into a local workflow that stays in your hands.

Your files are opened, rendered and edited in the browser. The workspace does not need an account, a server or a tracking pixel to do its job.

Open a file, make the change, save the result. The same engine powers the browser workspace and the native app, with a clear boundary between your documents and the network.

Read the boundary
AWhere are my PDFs processed?

In the lockmypdf workspace running in your browser. The normal local-file workflow does not upload the document to a lockmypdf server.

ACan I open a PDF from a URL?

Yes. Use a same-origin or CORS-enabled URL with the file query parameter when you intentionally want the browser to fetch a document.

ADo I need an account?

No. The browser workspace is designed to start without an account, a subscription or a cloud processing service.

AHow do I start?

Choose “Open a PDF” anywhere on this page, select a file, and use the document tools that appear in the workspace.
